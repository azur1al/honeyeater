Honeyeater Publications

About Us

Founded in 1992 by Melissa and Arthur Galbraith, Honeyeater Publications supports indie authors and writers to get their works to their desired audience. We have a focus on elevating voices, bringing emerging writers to the forefront and bringing wider audiences to our established authors.

As part of our goal to elevate these voices, we run an open writers group that meets every second Thursday evening. Please direct any enquiries about joining to their contact address: art.galbraith [at] outlook [dot] com.
